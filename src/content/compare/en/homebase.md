Homebase is a capable scheduling tool, with a free plan and an AI scheduling assistant on its higher plans. Homebase's marketing says the assistant uses availability and preferred shifts. Wiz Scheduler is built around a say for employees: preferred days, preferred hours, a weekly hour limit, and who they work well with or should be kept apart from go into the generator, and the week is built around them.

## How preferences work

Managers can enter preferences for everyone: preferred days, preferred time ranges, a weekly limit on hours, a cap on shifts in a time range, times someone can't work, and coworkers to work with, keep apart from, or never schedule together. Turn on preference requests in Company Settings and your employees can send their preferences for you to approve. Requests are off by default, and a manager approves each one and sets how much it counts.

A firm preference means the employee is not considered for a shift that breaks it, and the shift is left open if nobody else qualifies. A soft one makes the employee a less likely pick, and if they get the shift anyway it is marked with an asterisk. That is why there is less to fix: the preferences are already in the draft, and the places it couldn't meet them are marked.

Homebase's marketing says its AI scheduling assistant uses availability and preferred shifts. That assistant comes with Plus and above, so Essentials has no auto-scheduling.

## Pricing

Homebase charges per location, so the price grows with every site. Wiz Scheduler charges one base price for the company, with every location included.

## Where Homebase is stronger

- **A free plan.** Homebase Basic is free for {homebaseBasicLocations} location and up to {homebaseBasicEmployees} employees.
- **An AI scheduling assistant.** On Plus and above, Homebase's marketing says it uses availability and preferred shifts.
- **An annual discount.** Homebase's annual prices are lower per location. Wiz Scheduler has no annual discount.

If those are the reasons you use Homebase, it may well remain the better fit.
