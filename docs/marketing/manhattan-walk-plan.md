Field plan for getting the first Wiz Scheduler users by visiting restaurants, cafés and studios in Manhattan in person.

**Phone tracker (private artifact):** https://claude.ai/artifact/9y6yZrDdL9hCM3NzjMNp8i

The tracker holds the route checklists, a log of every stop, the follow-up list and the pitch script. Ticks and logged stops are saved to the account, not to this repo.

## Goal

- 120–160 walk-ins → 30–50 conversations with whoever makes the schedule → 3–6 businesses using it.
- Offer: free setup, plus the next 4 schedules built for them free. After that, $18/month.
- Ask staff "Who does the schedule here?" and get a name and when they're in.
